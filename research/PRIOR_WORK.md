# Is a single cited page a point of failure?

**Scope:** Given two annotated X posts connected through exact cited pages, classify the effect of removing one page: same distance, longer detour, or disconnection. The proposed contribution is the question and its empirical answer, not BFS or bipartite graphs.

| Closest public work located | Overlap | Difference from the proposed measurement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Who Checks the Checkers?](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s42001-025-00455-y) | Examines the sources used by contributors. | Source credibility and audience perception are different outcomes from reachability after deleting a cited page. |
| [Can Community Notes Replace Professional Fact-Checkers?](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.14132v2) | Connects annotated posts and cited fact-checking sources, including links to broader narratives. | Measures reliance on professional fact-checkers, not whether a particular page is necessary for a route. |
| [From Birdwatch to Community Notes](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.09585) | Reviews the literature, analyzes the network and reports how frequently source domains are referenced. | Several distinct articles share a publisher. A domain-frequency statistic cannot tell whether deleting a particular article severs a particular pair. |
| [Netzschleuder Community Notes graph](https://networks.skewed.de/net/community_notes) | Public, bipartite representation of the same platform. | Its two node classes are contributors and notes; its edges describe ratings, not post–page citations. |
| [CheckFirst's Community Notes Dashboard](https://github.com/CheckFirstHQ/X-Community-Notes-Dashboard) | Running dashboard for notes, external sources, and post-level search results. | Its documented source features rank domains and measure fact-checker usage; they do not offer page closure and post-pair rerouting. |
